I am an M.S.E. student in Applied Mathematics and Statistics at Johns Hopkins University. I currently work with [Prof. Mateo Díaz](https://mateodd25.github.io/) and [Prof. Liwei Jiang](https://liwei-jiang97.github.io/) on problems in stochastic optimization and statistical theory.

My research interests lie broadly in **optimization, statistics, and machine learning theory**. I am especially interested in problems where understanding the mathematical structure of a stochastic or statistical system clarifies what is fundamentally achievable and suggests the right algorithmic approach. I particularly enjoy questions where lower bounds and algorithm design inform one another: first characterizing the intrinsic difficulty of a problem, then asking whether an efficient method can attain that benchmark with finite-sample or time-uniform guarantees.

My current work studies nonasymptotic local minimax theory for decision-dependent stochastic optimization and anytime-valid guarantees for stochastic gradient methods. More broadly, I am interested in reliable learning and decision-making methods whose efficiency is accompanied by clear mathematical guarantees.
