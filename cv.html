Project: Nonasymptotic Local Minimax Bounds for Decision-Dependent Stochastic Optimization

  • Studying finite-sample statistical guarantees for decision-dependent stochastic optimization, where decisions influence the distribution of subsequent observations.
  • Developing and analyzing stochastic optimization algorithms for estimating equilibrium points, with the goal of attaining optimal local minimax squared-error rates.
